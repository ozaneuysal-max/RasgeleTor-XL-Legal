özelliklerini kapsar. Uygulamanın geliştiriciye ait kullanıcı hesabı, giriş

Mevcut sürüm geliştiriciye ait bir sunucuya kişisel veri, kullanım analitiği veya
reklam kimliği göndermez. Reklam, üçüncü taraf analiz, davranışsal takip veya
çökme raporlama SDK’sı içermez. Geliştirici kişisel verileri satmaz ve reklam
amacıyla paylaşmaz.

saklanabilir:

- son doğrulanan yerel Premium erişim durumu.

Bu içerikler geliştiriciye gönderilmez. Rastgele seçim ve sonuç üretme işlemleri
cihazda çalışır.

Premium, Google Play üzerinden sunulan tek seferlik ve tüketilmeyen bir
uygulama içi üründür. Ödeme, fiyatlandırma, vergi, makbuz ve mağaza hesabına
bağlı satın alma kaydı Google Play tarafından kendi şartları ve gizlilik
politikası uyarınca işlenir.

Uygulama, Premium erişimini açmak veya geri yüklemek için ürün kimliği ile satın
alma/yetki durumunu Google Play Billing arayüzü üzerinden cihazda işler.
Geliştirici tam ödeme kartı veya banka bilgilerinize erişmez ve satın alma
verilerini kendi sunucusunda toplamaz. Google Play’de tutulan işlem kaydı,
uygulama silinse bile mağaza hesabınızda kalabilir.

## English

### Scope

This policy covers the Randomizer XL mobile application, including its free and
Premium features. The app does not provide a developer-operated user account,
sign-in system, remote database, or user profile service.

### Data the developer does not collect

The current version does not send personal data, usage analytics, or advertising
identifiers to a developer-operated server. It contains no advertising,
third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, or crash reporting SDK. The
developer does not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

### Data processed only on your device

The following information may be processed and stored in the app’s private area
on your device:

- names, options, and custom lists that you enter;
- favorites, history, and recently used modes;
- language, theme, sound, vibration, and animation preferences;
- the last locally verified Premium entitlement state.

This content is not sent to the developer. Random selections and result
generation run on the device.

### Premium purchases

Premium is offered as a one-time, non-consumable in-app product through Google
Play. Google Play processes payment, pricing, tax, receipts, and the purchase
record associated with your store account under its own terms and privacy
policy.

To unlock or restore Premium, the app processes the product identifier and
purchase/entitlement status on the device through Google Play Billing. The
developer does not receive full payment card or bank details and does not
collect purchase data on a developer-operated server. A transaction record held
by Google Play may remain associated with your store account after the app is
removed.

### Sharing initiated by you

If you select “Share,” the app passes the result text to the operating system
share sheet. You choose the destination. Shared text may contain names or
options that you entered. The privacy policy of the selected third-party app
applies.

### Permissions and network use

The app does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, or
shared external storage. Core randomizer features work offline. A network
connection may be used for Premium purchases and restores through Google Play
and for external legal or support links that you choose to open.

### Health and fitness data

The Workout Picker may randomly suggest general exercises. The app does not
monitor, record, collect, or transmit health or fitness data and does not access
Health Connect, body sensors, or physical activity permissions.

### Retention and deletion

Local information may remain on your device until you delete relevant history or
lists, clear app data through system settings, or uninstall the app. There is no
developer account or server account to delete. Google Play purchase records are
managed through your Google account and Google’s retention practices.

### Security

Local information is held in the operating system’s app-specific storage. No
technical method can guarantee absolute security; device lock settings,
operating system updates, and people with access to your device may affect
locally stored information.

### Children’s privacy

Randomizer XL is a general-audience entertainment and decision tool and is not
specifically directed to children under 13. The developer does not collect
personal data from children or other users through the app.

### Third-party services

Premium transactions are subject to the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/).

### Changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be
updated. For privacy questions, email `ozaneuysal@gmail.com`.
